# On Board: Doctrine

**D1. On Board stays the 2019 team's game: we bring it up to date and fix what is broken, we do not rewrite what they made.**
The team's hints, cage lines, art and puzzles stay theirs. Lines an agent writes are placeholders until Simon keeps them.

**D2. The game never gives the answer and never judges the choice: a hint is Ross's state of mind, and both uses of an item have a cost.**
A highlight lights every item, not only the one that fits. No ending calls one path good.

**D3. The art is shown the way the artists painted it: never stretched, squashed or blurred to suit the code.**
When code and art disagree on a size or a shape, the code moves.

**D4. Gentle over loud: nothing flashes, blares or shakes harder than the moment needs.**

**D5. On Board will never be sold: free assets are welcome when their licence allows it, and credit goes where the licence asks for it.**

**D6. Working on the game never disturbs Simon: no window on his screen and no sound in his speakers unless he starts it.**
